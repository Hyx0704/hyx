Platform Privacy Commitment

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Application notice:
  • Local storage: Data you create in the app (including settings and content) is stored on your device.
  • No sign-in required: The app does not offer account registration or login and does not collect credentials used to identify you.
  • Offline use: Core features work without a network connection. This privacy page is a static document you can read without being online after it is opened.
  • No upload of your content: The app does not upload content you create or edit on your device to our servers or third-party servers.

Overview

This Commitment explains how we handle personal information across this platform application. Subject to the local-storage and no-upload practices above, we process only limited information when needed for security and legal compliance.

Collection Scope

The app is designed to run primarily on your device: content you enter or save locally is not uploaded to servers. Where operation requires it, the device may keep necessary local status or logs for troubleshooting and stability; we do not bulk-upload your personal content to the cloud. We do not collect personally identifiable information for advertising without your informed consent.

Where the platform collects device identifiers, usage analytics, or operational logs needed for service operation, such processing is limited to what is necessary for that operation and does not include uploading your on-device content.

Use of Information

Information is used to maintain functionality, prevent abuse, improve experience, and fulfill legal obligations. When you use the app fully offline, related processing stays on your device where applicable.

Access Restrictions

Only authorized personnel and processors with a legitimate need may access personal data we are required to hold. This does not extend to content you keep on your device and have not uploaded.

Protection Measures

Encryption, authentication, and continuous security oversight are applied to reduce risk. Please also secure your device so others cannot access local app data without authorization.

No Sale of Data

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Your Choices

You may withdraw consent where applicable, adjust permissions, or submit privacy requests. Because data is stored mainly on your device, you may also delete local content by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Third Parties

External components are governed by their own privacy policies. If you choose network-dependent third-party services, those services apply separately. The app does not upload local content to third parties on your behalf without your action.

Policy Maintenance

We review and update this Commitment periodically to reflect practice or legal changes. Refer to the effective date at the top of this page for the current version.
